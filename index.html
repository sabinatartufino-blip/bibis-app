Your daily targets

Fill these in and your targets appear here.

  • Scan it, photograph it, or type it A barcode goes to Open Food Facts. A photo of the nutrition table gets read for you — including whether the label counts per 100 g or per serving. Typing always works.
  • Save a meal once, log it in one tap A breakfast bowl of eleven ingredients gets entered once, not every morning.
  • Nothing leaves this phone No account, no server, no analytics. Export a backup now and then — it is the only copy.

Targets are a starting point from a standard equation (Mifflin–St Jeor, minus 10 %), with protein set high at 2 g per kg for holding muscle. Change any of them in Settings. This is not medical advice — if you have a condition that affects what you eat, take these to someone qualified.

Food databases

USDA FoodData Central is the reference table for whole foods — kiwi, oats, chicken breast. The key is free and takes a minute. Without it, name searches fall back to the vision model below, or to Open Food Facts.

Label reading

Photographing a nutrition label sends that one image to a vision model, which returns the values and the basis they apply to. The same key answers name searches when USDA has no match. Your keys are stored only on this phone and never leave it except to call the API you chose.

Your data

Everything lives in this browser's storage on this phone. Nothing is uploaded, so a backup is the only copy that survives a lost phone — export now and then.

Share your ingredients

Sends the ingredient list only — names, brands, barcodes and nutrition values. No log, no weights, no targets, no profile, no keys. Saved meals are left out because they point at ingredients on this phone.

Importing adds to your library and never removes or changes what is already there. Anything you already have under the same name and brand is skipped, so the same file can be imported twice with no duplicates.

Messaging apps are fussy about files. If one will not carry it, send the list as text instead — copy it, paste it into the chat, and the other person pastes it below.
